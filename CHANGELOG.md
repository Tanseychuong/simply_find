# Changelog

## Unreleased: backend foundation

- Built from the EEL backend: premium tiers removed, roles limited to internships, graduate programmes, entry-level roles (at most 2 years' experience) and optional graduate school listings.
- Upload gate for every file: real file type, PDF scripts refused, photo metadata removed, ClamAV scan.
- Sign-in hardening: confirmed email to apply, HttpOnly cookie sessions with CSRF, refresh token revocation, two-step sign-in for staff and company teams, applicant contacts hidden until shortlisted.
- Student profile in four steps, document vault, certificate links with Open Badge verification, eligibility rules, apply with vault documents, access log of who opened a student's files.
- Recruiting tools: applicant filters, bulk status changes, CSV and printable exports with an export log.
- Recruitment drives for the South Sudan pilot: drive page and QR code, desk signups with consent, claim by email, unclaimed accounts deleted after 14 days.
